# 30-Day AI Fluency Course for Business Leaders
*One topic per day · ~45–60 minutes daily · Headings only — use each day's title as your study prompt*

---

## Week 1 — Modern AI Foundations & Jargon Decoder

**Day 1:** How LLMs Actually Work — Tokens, Parameters, Training vs Inference (just enough depth to speak credibly)

**Day 2:** The Jargon Map — GenAI vs Agentic AI vs Traditional ML vs Deep Learning: What's What and What's Hype

**Day 3:** Context Windows, Prompts, and Why "Prompt Engineering" Became "Context Engineering"

**Day 4:** The Model Landscape 2026 — Frontier Models (Claude, GPT, Gemini), Open-Weight Models (Llama, Mistral, DeepSeek), and How to Compare Them

**Day 5:** Multimodal AI — Text, Vision, Voice, Video: What's Production-Ready vs Demo-Ware

**Day 6:** Reasoning Models & Test-Time Compute — Why "Thinking" Models Changed the Economics

**Day 7:** Weekly Review + Hands-On — Spend the Day Using 2–3 Frontier Chatbots on Real Work Tasks

---

## Week 2 — Building AI Applications (The Builder's Vocabulary)

**Day 8:** APIs, System Prompts, and Temperature — The Anatomy of an AI-Powered Application

**Day 9:** RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) — Why It's the #1 Enterprise Pattern

**Day 10:** Embeddings & Vector Databases — How Machines "Search by Meaning"

**Day 11:** Prompting vs RAG vs Fine-Tuning — The Decision Framework Every Leader Should Know

**Day 12:** Tool Use / Function Calling — How LLMs Take Actions, Not Just Generate Text

**Day 13:** MCP (Model Context Protocol) & A2A — The New Connectivity Standards for AI Systems

**Day 14:** AI Agents & Orchestration — Single Agents, Multi-Agent Systems, and Frameworks (LangGraph, CrewAI, Claude Agent SDK)

---

## Week 3 — From Prototype to Production (Where Most Projects Die)

**Day 15:** Evals — How You Actually Measure Whether an AI System Works

**Day 16:** Hallucinations, Grounding & Guardrails — Managing the Reliability Problem

**Day 17:** AI Security — Prompt Injection, Data Leakage, and the OWASP Top 10 for LLMs

**Day 18:** Tokenomics — Understanding AI Costs, Pricing Models, Caching, and Model Right-Sizing

**Day 19:** Observability & LLMOps — Monitoring, Tracing, and Versioning AI in Production

**Day 20:** The 79%-Adopted-but-11%-in-Production Gap — Why Pilots Fail and What Successful Deployments Share

**Day 21:** Weekly Review + Hands-On — Build a Simple No-Code/Low-Code AI Workflow (Claude Projects, Custom GPTs, or n8n/Zapier)

---

## Week 4 — Enterprise Adoption & AI Strategy

**Day 22:** Use-Case Discovery — Identifying High-Volume, Well-Defined Workflows Where AI Wins First

**Day 23:** Build vs Buy vs Partner — Platforms, Copilots, Vertical AI Vendors, and Custom Builds

**Day 24:** Data Readiness — Why Your Data Estate Decides Your AI Ceiling

**Day 25:** AI Governance & Regulation — EU AI Act, Responsible AI, Audit Trails, Human-in-the-Loop

**Day 26:** Sovereign AI, Edge AI & AI Factories — The 2026 Infrastructure Trends and When They Matter

**Day 27:** Measuring ROI — From Productivity Claims to Real P&L Impact (and the 49% Average ROI Debate)

**Day 28:** Change Management & Workforce — Reskilling, AI-Augmented Roles, and Org Design for AI-Native Companies

**Day 29:** Writing an AI Strategy — Vision, Use-Case Portfolio, Operating Model, Risk Posture (Capstone Prep)

**Day 30:** Capstone — Draft a 1-Page AI Adoption Plan for Your Own Business Domain and Pressure-Test It with an AI Assistant

---

## How to Use This Plan
- Each day, ask an AI assistant: *"Teach me [day's topic] as if I'm a business leader with basic AI knowledge. Use examples, define every jargon term, and end with 3 questions to test me."*
- Days 7, 21, and 30 are hands-on — doing beats reading.
- Keep a running glossary of terms you encounter; by Day 30 you'll have your own jargon dictionary.
